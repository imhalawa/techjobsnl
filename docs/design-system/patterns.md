# Experience patterns

## Company identity

Show the canonical company name and follow state consistently. Official links are visually distinct from collected external
coverage. Company scale and industry are researched context, not live audited facts.

## Feed update

Every Feed row communicates company, update type, headline, observed or published time, unread/change state, and origin.
Selection reveals evidence without replacing the chronological context.

## Company Profile

Profiles progress from identity to current activity, Jobs, Articles, technology landscape, and Trends. Empty sections explain
whether no evidence exists, collection has not run, a source is unavailable, or the capability belongs to a later release.

## Evidence and confidence

Evidence-bearing results expose source, observation time, extractor version when applicable, and a direct inspection path.
Confidence uses text and iconography in addition to color. Insufficient evidence is a first-class result.

## AiExperience

Use the violet AiExperience identity and name the capability being used. Show deterministic content as the stable base,
identify generated or refined output, provide recovery when the model is unavailable, and require review before durable
canonical or preference state changes.

## Operational states

- **Loading:** preserve layout and current trusted content where possible.
- **Empty:** explain the next useful action without implying failure.
- **Incomplete:** retain trusted content and state what could not be proven.
- **Failed:** preserve prior data, explain scope, and offer a safe retry.
- **Offline:** distinguish unavailable external refresh from fully available local browsing.
- **Disabled:** explain why a capability or source is unavailable.

## Accessibility baseline

All primary journeys support keyboard operation, visible focus, meaningful screen-reader labels, non-color state cues,
scalable text, reduced motion, and layouts that remain usable at the release's declared minimum size.
