Find Netherlands tech jobs by the skills they mention

TechJobsNL is an open-source Rust terminal job search tool. It scans official company career sources, stores job history locally, and connects skill demand back to real vacancies.

View source on GitHub

Why TechJobsNL?

  • Search real company vacancies
  • Discover roles through required skills
  • Keep your job data local with SQLite
  • Explore evidence-linked analytics
