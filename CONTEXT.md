# TechJobsNL

TechJobsNL helps a person follow technology employers and stay current through locally retained, evidence-linked company
intelligence.

## Language

**Company Profile**:
The durable identity and current vacancy activity for one employer, including follow state and official links.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, company page

**Company Follow**:
A person's explicit choice to include a company in their personalized updates.
_Avoid_: Subscription, favorite

**Company Update**:
A dated, evidence-linked vacancy change concerning a company, such as a new, changed, reopened, or closed vacancy.
_Avoid_: Post, notification

**Feed**:
The personalized chronological collection of Company Updates from followed companies.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, timeline

**Vacancy**:
An employment opportunity published by a company or its authorized applicant-tracking system.
_Avoid_: Job item, posting
